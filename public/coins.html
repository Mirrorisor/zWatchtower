      How the flags work

        Cards refresh a few dozen coins at a time, so each shows when it was last read. "Top 10" leaves out pools, bonding curves and other program accounts. Where Sapling's trade history doesn't reach back to a coin's launch, launch facts are left blank rather than guessed. A coin's history is read once and kept, so a long one fills in over a few runs.

        Source: zWatchtower on GitHub (coins-check.mjs). Raw data: coins.json.
