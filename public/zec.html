Backing

ZEC on Solana is listed by Messari and Blockworks as bridged by NEAR's Omni Bridge, so it most likely comes out of the NEAR supply: don't add the two together. Whether the ZEC on Zcash behind these tokens covers them isn't checked yet: the bridge doesn't publish the Zcash addresses that hold it. That's the next thing zWatchtower is working on.
